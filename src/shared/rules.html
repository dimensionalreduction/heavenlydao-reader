My site rules

The sites the extension knows come built in. Add a rule here to read any other site: it says where the chapter text is and, if you like, where the chapter list and the book's details are. A rule is only selectors (nothing in it is run), and it can only read pages of its own site, with your login there. Add rules for sites you trust. A rule you add for a built-in site is used instead of the built-in one.
